03 — HOW TO PLAY

Move, wait, get home.

Simple rules, combinations that make you think. Every action brings the next season closer.

  1. 01 ↗

    Move the fox

    Swipe in a direction or tap a neighbouring cell.

  2. 02 ❄

    The season changes

    Every few actions the next season arrives and seasonal terrain transforms.

  3. 03 ⧖

    Wait when you need to

    Sometimes the best move is WAIT: let time pass and the path opens.

  4. 04 ⌂

    Reach the den

    Collect the acorns and finish in as few actions as you can for three stars.
