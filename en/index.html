03 — ABOUT US

We build mobile products
we want to use.

GoByde is an independent mobile game and app studio. We care about clear ideas, good controls and products that do not waste your time.

Aura Clicker is our first game, and Turning Tails is our second. Over time, we want to make more small, well-made mobile products that are easy to understand.
